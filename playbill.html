Midnight Shadows — Performance Program

Printing Instructions

This program is designed to print as a folded booklet.

  • Paper size: Letter (8.5” x 11”)
  • Print on both sides
  • Flip on short edge
  • Use “Booklet” setting if available

After printing, fold the pages in half and staple along the spine to create the final 5.5” x 8.5” program.
